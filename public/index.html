Our Aim & Goals

🎯

Aim

To remove the information gap that holds Indian students back — by turning scattered, hard-to-find college, career, and scholarship data into clear, personalised guidance.

🚀

Goal 1 — Access

Make quality academic guidance available to students in tier-2/3 cities, not just metro coaching hubs.

🧠

Goal 2 — Intelligence

Use AI (Veda) to answer real student questions in Hindi, English, Bengali & Tamil — with your own marks and goals in context.

💸

Goal 3 — Affordability

Keep the core experience free, and surface genuine scholarships & loans so money is never the reason a student stops studying.

Crack JEE 2026: A 6-Month Study Roadmap

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Learnify ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, password (hashed), and optional profile details (school, board, state, target exam).
  • Document & academic data: marksheets / result files you upload, and extracted marks we parse to personalise guidance.
  • Usage data: chats with Veda, tool usage, and basic analytics to improve the product.

2. How We Use Your Data

To provide personalised answers, match scholarships, build study plans, and keep the service secure. We never sell your personal data.

3. Document & Academic Data

Uploaded files and extracted marks are stored in our Supabase database and used only to personalise your experience (e.g., Veda answering about your marks). You can delete documents anytime from your profile.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — database & authentication hosting.
  • Groq — AI inference that powers Veda's responses.
  • Razorpay — payment processing for Premium (we do not store card details).

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local storage to remember your session and last-opened tab. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. Delete your account or individual documents anytime; we remove associated data on request.

7. Your Rights

You may access, correct, export, or delete your data. Contact us at shubham.mallick1440@gmail.com.
